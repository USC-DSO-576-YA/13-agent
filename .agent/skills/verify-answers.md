# Skill: Verify answers

1. Solve the question from its stated inputs before consulting the proposed answer.
2. Compare the independently derived answer with the proposed key and grading criteria. Investigate discrepancies and check that the criteria accept valid alternatives.
3. Check assumptions, units, data types, boundary cases, and acceptable alternative answers where relevant.
4. For multiple choice, inspect every option for unintended correctness.
5. Read the cited course material to verify that the methods and difficulty are within scope.
6. Ensure the question actually tests its assigned objective and does not reveal the solution through wording or another question.
7. Record verification methods and unresolved issues. Approve only fully checked questions; otherwise request a concrete revision.

Use executable checks when available and relevant. Distinguish tool-verified results from manual reasoning.
